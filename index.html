The SADTM campus of Jaipur National University is well-connected by air, rail and road. The nearest airport is Jaipur International Airport, around 30 km from the campus. The nearest railway station is Jaipur Junction, which is approximately 25 km away. Regular buses, cabs and private transport options are easily available to reach the campus.
The School of Engineering and Technology (SOET) at JNU fosters an innovation-driven, multidisciplinary learning environment. With cutting-edge laboratories, project-based curriculum, industry certifications, and continuous guidance from experienced faculty and researchers, students gain both strong conceptual fundamentals and hands-on exposure to emerging technologies.
Jaipur National University maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging in any form, compliant with UGC and Supreme Court regulations. The campus is monitored by dedicated anti-ragging committees, squad patrols, CCTV surveillance, and a 24x7 student helpline to ensure a safe, inclusive, and welcoming atmosphere for every new student.

JNU provides separate, secure, and modern on-campus hostels for boys and girls.
